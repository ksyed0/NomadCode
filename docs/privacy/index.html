Policy

We built NomadCode to run on your device. Your code is yours — here's exactly what happens to your data.

Effective 3 May 2026 NomadCode v1.0 Last updated 3 May 2026
01 · Overview

The short version

NomadCode is an offline-first mobile IDE. We do not operate servers that store your code, your files, or your identity. Your code lives on your device.

No analytics. No advertising. No crash reporting in v1.0. The only data that leaves your device is what you explicitly choose to share — when using AI completions, pushing to GitHub, or managing your subscription.

02 · Data Collection

What data we collect

NomadCode collects the minimum data necessary to provide its features. Here is everything, with no omissions:

  • GitHub OAuth token — when you sign in with GitHub, we receive an OAuth access token. This token is stored exclusively in your device's secure keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). It is never transmitted to FableSoft servers.
  • GitHub profile information — your GitHub username and avatar are stored locally to display in the app. We do not store or transmit your email address, repositories list, or any other GitHub data.
  • AI prompt content — when you use AI completions or chat (Pro+AI plan, or with your own API key), the content of your active file or selected text is sent to the AI provider you have selected. See Section 04 for full details.
  • Subscription status — an anonymised app user ID is shared with RevenueCat to manage your subscription entitlements. This ID is not linked to your name, email, or GitHub account. See Section 05.
  • App settings and preferences — theme, font size, editor configuration, and AI provider settings are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device.
03 · What we don't collect

Data we never collect

  • Your name, email address, or physical location
  • The contents of files you do not actively send to an AI provider
  • Usage analytics, session recordings, or behavioural tracking
  • Crash reports or error logs (crash reporting is planned for a future release and will be disclosed when added)
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID) — NomadCode contains no advertising SDKs
  • Any data from minors — NomadCode is rated 4+ / Everyone but is designed for developers
04 · AI Features

AI completions and your file content

This section requires careful reading if you use NomadCode's AI features.

When you use AI completions or the AI chat panel, the content of your active file is sent over the internet to the AI provider you have selected. Do not use AI features with files containing passwords, API keys, personal data, or any content you do not wish to share with a third-party AI service.

NomadCode uses OpenRouter as its built-in AI provider for Pro+AI subscribers. OpenRouter routes your requests to the model you have selected (which may include models operated by Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral, or others). Each provider has its own privacy policy governing how they handle inference data.

If you use the BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) feature on the Pro plan, your requests are sent directly to the provider you configure — FableSoft is not involved in that data path.

  • NomadCode does not log, store, or read the content of your AI requests
  • API keys you enter for BYOK are stored in your device's secure keychain only — never in our systems
  • The built-in OpenRouter key used for Pro+AI is an application-level secret and is never shared with users
  • You can disable all AI features at any time in Settings → AI
05 · Subscriptions

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

NomadCode uses RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements across iOS and Android. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymised app user ID generated by RevenueCat (not linked to your name or email)
  • Your subscription status and entitlement level (Free, Pro, or Pro+AI)
  • Platform-level purchase receipts (processed by Apple or Google, not FableSoft)

FableSoft does not receive or store your payment card details. All payment processing is handled by Apple (iOS) or Google (Android) under their respective payment policies.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

07 · Storage & Security

How your data is stored

  • Device keychain — GitHub OAuth token and any BYOK API keys are stored in iOS Keychain / Android Keystore. This storage is encrypted at rest by the operating system.
  • Local file system — your code files are stored in the application sandbox on your device. On iOS this is the app's Documents directory; on Android it is equivalent sandboxed storage. Files are not synced to FableSoft servers.
  • AsyncStorage — app settings and preferences are stored in AsyncStorage (encrypted on supported devices). This data does not leave your device.
  • No FableSoft servers — we do not operate databases or storage infrastructure for NomadCode. There is no FableSoft cloud to breach.
08 · Your Rights

Your rights and controls

Because NomadCode stores data locally on your device, you have direct control:

  • Delete all app data — uninstalling NomadCode from your device removes all locally stored data including settings, cached files, and the OAuth token from your keychain.
  • Revoke GitHub access — you can revoke NomadCode's GitHub OAuth token at any time from github.com → Settings → Applications → Authorized OAuth Apps.
  • Cancel your subscription — manage or cancel your subscription through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period.
  • Disable AI features — turn off all AI completions and chat in Settings → AI without affecting any other app functionality.
09 · Children

Children's privacy

NomadCode is designed for software developers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information through our app, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it promptly.

10 · Updates

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when features that affect data handling are added (such as crash reporting or analytics, if introduced in a future version). The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of NomadCode after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Material changes — such as introducing a new category of data collection — will be announced via an in-app notice on the next app launch after the change takes effect.
